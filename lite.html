FAQ

Before you subscribe

How do I get Lite after paying?

Sign in to the store with Discord and check out. Lite unlocks on that Discord account right away. Open the Frost Launcher, sign in with the same Discord account and pick a Lite instance.

What do I need to use Lite?

A Minecraft: Java Edition account, a Discord account and the free Frost Launcher on Windows, macOS or Linux. Lite instances run Minecraft 1.21.11, with an OpenGL and a Vulkan build.

How can I pay?

By debit or credit card, Apple Pay or Google Pay. The payment runs through Whop, a payment platform, so Frost never sees or stores your card details. Prices are in euros and your bank converts them if you use another currency. Want to pay with PayPal? Open a ticket on our Discord and we will set it up for you.

Can I get Lite without a subscription?

Yes. "Buy 30 days" under the plans is one €2.90 payment for 30 days of Lite on your own Discord account. Nothing renews and nothing needs cancelling; Lite simply ends after 30 days unless you buy again. It has no free trial, and it is not available while you have an active subscription.

Can I buy Lite for someone else?

Yes. "Buy a Lite gift" under the plans is a single €2.90 payment for 30 days of Lite. The buyer signs in with Discord and gets a link; whoever opens it and signs in with Discord gets Lite on their account. Nothing renews. A parent can pay with their card while the player is signed in, and cloaks can be gifted the same way from each cloak's page. Discount codes do not apply to gifts.

What happens after the free trial?

The trial on the monthly plan lasts 3 days and starts the moment you check out. If you keep it, it continues at €2.90 a month. Cancel before the 3 days end and you pay nothing.

How do I cancel?

Anytime on the cancel page. There is no minimum period on the monthly plan.

Can I use a creator or discount code?

Yes. Put Lite in your cart, type the code into the discount field and it is carried over to the checkout. The Black Friday sale on cloaks and collections applies by itself.

Can I buy Lite and cloaks together?

They are two separate payments. Check out Lite first: your cloaks stay saved in the cart and get the Lite member discount when you pay for them right after.

Can I switch from monthly to annual?

Yes. Sign in, and the Annual card above turns into a "Switch to annual" button.

Is regular Frost still free?

Yes. Frost Client stays free. Lite is an optional upgrade for players who want every frame and the extra perks.
